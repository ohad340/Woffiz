Status: slices **3A (foundations), 3B (admin review, publication, withdrawal, suspension) 3C (technique-bound generation) and 3D (owner playback, suspension guard)** implemented on branch feature/phase-3a-media-foundations; 3E: the automated playback verification is done (docs/MEDIA_QA.md, decision 84); real-device QA, sound and narration review and mandatory coverage are not started. The pilot production package is docs/PILOT_MOUTHING_STANDARD.md: footage is generated by the owner in ChatGPT through Higgsfield (decision 87, superseding the vendor parts of decision 85); the vendor proposals in section B below are obsolete. ## Context
Phases 0–2 are on `main`. Every exercise shows a placeholder (`src/components/media/VideoUnavailable.tsx`).
- **Media today:** an isolated test catalog. `MediaAsset` has `id`, `family`, `variant`, `status='TEST'` and `isTestFixture`, and `src/server/ai/media.ts` offers it only with `AI_TEST_MEDIA` (local or tests). The production catalog is empty, so every production `ExerciseVersion.mediaId` is null.
- **Matching today:** a topic-level check of `mediaId` in `src/server/ai/validate.ts`.
- **Storage and admin:** no object storage. `Role.ADMIN` exists but nothing checks it.

Goal: reviewed, reusable AI-generated demonstration clips with Hebrew narration. Each clip is bound exactly to a versioned technique definition. Personalized plans, progression rules, authentication, AI cost limits and the existing Hebrew RTL design all stay as they are.

### Owner decisions (2026-11-02)
1. **Review.** No certified trainer, trainer accounts, certification fields or signatures. The owner, as ADMIN, reviews each video against its exercise instructions before publication.
2. **Sources.** Each technique records its sources: URL, publisher, verification date and the exact instructions they support. A boundary is final only after the owner has read the actual page. URL existence or tag coverage proves nothing. Boundaries that no source supports are marked product defaults and need an explicit owner decision.
3. **Internal only.** Sources and review records stay in the admin workflow. No badges, banners, endorsement claims or sourcing text appear in the app. Unrelated existing disclosures stay.
4. **Withdrawal by reason.** A technical withdrawal leaves the written instructions available. A safety suspension pauses the guidance without rewriting history.
5. **Hebrew voice guidance** in every clip (this replaces the "silent first batch" idea).

## A. Software slices (sequential; tests ship inside each slice)

### 3A — Foundations (implement now, locally)
1. **Technique catalog** (`src/domain/techniques/`)
   - Each definition is identified by `key@version` and is immutable once released. A change to the demonstrated behaviour creates a new version; old versions are kept.
   - Each definition has:
     - `topic`, `review.status` (`draft` or `approved`, plus owner and date) and `sources[]`, each `{url, publisher, title, verifiedOn, supports[], summary}`.
     - `audience`: a minimum age band, with its basis.
     - Per-variant (STANDARD and optionally EASIER) **demonstration core**:
       - mandatory steps in order, each with an id, Hebrew text, an action id and an optional audible speech cue (`name` or `praise`);
       - critical parameters: name, unit, integer range, default and basis (source or product default);
       - a mastery-criterion template;
       - optional catalog-approved supporting tips;
       - preparation items.
   - `fingerprintTechnique()` is a SHA-256 of canonical JSON. `techniques.lock.json` freezes released fingerprints, and a test fails if a released definition changes.
   - `renderTechniqueContent(def, variant, params, tipIds)` builds the standard `ExerciseContent` from the catalog. The mandatory steps and the criterion are never AI text.
   - `validateTechniqueParams` checks integers and ranges deterministically.
   - Initial definitions: `mouthing-redirect-toy@1` and `name-look@1`, both based on Dogs Trust pages read in full. They stay **draft** until the owner approves their product-default boundaries.
   - Not implemented yet: toilet, settle, alone and sit. Their sources have only been seen in search results.
2. **Schema and migration** (applied to the local databases only). New tables, enums and columns are listed in "Schema" below. Raw SQL adds:
   - the immutability trigger on `MediaVersion` (content columns frozen, no DELETE, only these status moves: DRAFT→REVIEWED→PUBLISHED→WITHDRAWN and DRAFT or REVIEWED→WITHDRAWN);
   - append-only triggers on `MediaReview` and `TechniqueSuspensionEvent`;
   - a partial unique index allowing one PUBLISHED version per slot;
   - a CHECK that fixture objects live under `fixtures/` and that a production version (`isTestFixture = false`) has audio;
   - RLS enabled and `anon`/`authenticated` revoked on the new tables.
3. **Bundle validation** (`src/server/media/`), all pure and unit-tested:
   - **MP4 box parser.** Checks `ftyp`, faststart (`moov` before `mdat`), the `mvhd` duration, a `vide` track (avc1) and a `soun` track (mp4a).
   - **WebP poster.** Magic bytes and size.
   - **WebVTT parser.** Hebrew cues, no tags, cues in order without overlap and inside the duration, at most 2 lines of 42 characters.
   - **Manifest** (zod).
     - Technique: `key@version`, variant, and its `techniqueFingerprint`, which must equal the catalog's.
     - Timeline: the action segments in exactly the core's step order.
     - Voice segments: `narrator` or `demo`. Segments never overlap, so there are no competing voices. Each demo segment sits inside the time window of a core step that declares a speech cue, and every speech cue has one.
     - Production record (always "AI-generated"): video and voice vendor, model and date.
   - **Captions and transcript.** Every voice segment's text appears in a caption cue that overlaps it in time. The transcript's speech entries equal the voice segments in order, with optional visual-description entries.
   - **Bundle fingerprint** (`media-bundle-v1`): a SHA-256 over the video, poster, captions and transcript hashes, the action timeline, the voice segments and the exact technique `key@version`, variant and fingerprint.
4. **Storage adapter** (`src/server/media/store.ts`), with `put` and `signedReadUrl`.
   - `local`: files under `.media-local/` with HMAC-signed, expiring URLs. It is allowed only when `isLocalMachine` or under NODE_ENV=test. The dev route that serves Range requests comes in 3D.
   - `supabase`: the Storage REST API through `fetch`, no SDK, a private bucket and server-only `SUPABASE_URL`, `SUPABASE_SECRET_KEY` and `MEDIA_BUCKET`. It is unit-tested with mocked `fetch` and never called in this step; the exact endpoints are re-checked against the docs before 3D's hosted use.
5. **Admin authorization.**
   - `requireAdminFromRequest` (route handlers) and `requireAdminOrNotFound` (pages) re-read `role` from the **database** each time and also require a verified email.
   - `scripts/grant-admin.ts --email … [--revoke]` sets the role locally, against whichever `DATABASE_URL` is in use.
6. **Ingest.** `ingestBundle()` with the CLI `scripts/media-ingest.ts <folder> [--fixture]`.
   - It validates everything, uploads under `v/{versionId}/` (or `fixtures/{versionId}/`) and creates a **DRAFT** `MediaVersion` with its fingerprints.
   - It upserts `TechniqueVersionState` and the slot `MediaAsset`.
   - `--fixture` is refused outside a local machine or tests.
7. **Docs.**
   - DECISIONS rows 58–66.
   - PRD §6, §7 and §12: owner review and verified sources replace the qualified reviewer; narration added.
   - ROADMAP Phase 3 inputs and acceptance.
   - DESIGN_SYSTEM video row: static loading text (no looping spinner), native audio controls.
   - `docs/PHASE_3_PLAN.md`: this plan.
   - `.env.example`.
8. **Tests.** Unit tests for the catalog (lock, fingerprint, render, params, source shape), MP4, VTT, manifest, captions and transcript, bundle, store and admin guards. Integration tests (PGlite locally) for the triggers, the publish index, fixture isolation, admin role-from-DB, ingest → DRAFT, and the Data API lock on the new tables.

### 3B — Review and publication (admin)
- **Pages.** `/admin/media` and `/admin/media/[versionId]`, using existing components:
  - draft preview with the native player, captions and transcript;
  - the technique core next to the action and voice timeline;
  - the sources and their verification notes;
  - the TECHNICAL and CONTENT checklists.
- **Lifecycle service.** Every transition takes the technique lock (`TechniqueVersionState` `FOR UPDATE`).
  - **REVIEWED** needs the latest TECHNICAL and CONTENT reviews to be APPROVED with `reviewedFingerprint = bundleFingerprint`.
  - **PUBLISHED** needs REVIEWED and an **approved** technique definition, and must not be a fixture outside local or tests. It withdraws the previous published version of the slot as SUPERSEDED.
  - **Content changes** mean a new ingest, a new version and fresh reviews.
- **Withdraw.** Takes a reason (`SUPERSEDED`, `TECHNICAL` or `SAFETY`) and a note. SAFETY also suspends the technique version (state row plus an event). Lifting a suspension is explicit.
- **Tests.** Permissions, gates, fingerprint mismatch, concurrent publish (PostgreSQL only), fixture refusal.

### 3C — Generation integration (catalog-rendered instructions)
- **Two level kinds** in the AI output:
  - **technique-bound**: `{technique:{key,version,variant}, params, tipIds, personalNote}`. The server renders the content. The AI may choose the order, technique, variant, parameters within range and catalog tips, plus one personal note (at most 140 characters, Hebrew, banned-wording and per-technique forbidden checks, no digits). The note is shown as supporting text and never as a step.
  - **free-form**: today's schema, unchanged. Allowed only while `MEDIA_REQUIRED=false`, so production keeps working with an empty catalog.
- **Offered catalog.** Approved, non-suspended technique versions that have a published, non-fixture STANDARD version. Fixtures are included only with `testMediaEnabled`.
- **Commit.** The commit transaction locks `TechniqueVersionState` rows `FOR SHARE` (in sorted order, after `lockPlan`) and rechecks publication and suspension. If anything changed, the job settles REJECTED with `media-unavailable` and the existing refund applies.
- **Versions and evaluation.** `PROMPT_VERSION` becomes `prompts-v3` and `POLICY_VERSION` becomes `policy-v2`. The recorded and fake evaluation cases are updated. A live evaluation makes paid API calls and needs the owner's OK.
- **Tests.** Parameter rejection, unknown or unapproved technique, the free-form fallback while the catalog is empty, the commit race with a withdrawal (PostgreSQL), and unchanged quota accounting.

### 3D — Exercise experience and safety suspension
- **Resolver.** `resolveDemo(exerciseVersion)` gives `none` (legacy or free-form), `paused`, `ready` (the published version in the exact `key@version`+variant slot) or `unavailable`. It writes nothing.
- **Guard.** `assertGuidanceAvailable`, inside the **same transaction** as attempt and mastery writes, after `lockPlan`, reading `TechniqueVersionState` `FOR SHARE`. A suspended technique gives `GUIDANCE_PAUSED`.
- **Lock order** everywhere: plan → technique states, sorted. Publish and suspend lock only technique states, so there are no cycles.
- **Replacement after a suspension.** Adapt on the current level is allowed without the two-struggle rule, but **only** if an eligible replacement exists (an approved, published, non-suspended technique of the same topic). That is checked **before** the job, the quota and the AI call. With no replacement there is an honest unavailable state and no adapt button.
- **Player** (`DemoVideo`):
  - native `<video controls preload="none" playsInline poster>` with a Hebrew captions track (same-origin `GET /api/media/versions/[id]/captions`);
  - playback starts only on a tap, with the native volume and mute controls;
  - aspect ratio taken from the metadata;
  - a transcript disclosure;
  - a static loading label;
  - on error, one signed-URL refresh, then the error state with the transcript and "לנסות שוב";
  - `unavailable` and `paused` states;
  - the easier-variant sheet shows the EASIER clip.
- **Local media route.** Range requests, HMAC token.
- **Tests:**
  - concurrency (PostgreSQL): suspension against mastery, suspension against a generation commit;
  - playback makes **no progression writes and no AI calls** (static import rule plus an e2e assertion that no attempts or mastery endpoint is hit and no job is created); authorized media reads and URL refreshes are expected;
  - captions track and keyboard transcript;
  - error and expired-URL recovery;
  - axe at 360, 768 and 1280 px, RTL, reduced motion;
  - existing plans unchanged.

### 3E — Real-device QA and rollout
- `docs/MEDIA_QA.md` matrix:
  - Devices: iPhone Safari (inline and fullscreen), Android Chrome, desktop Chrome, Firefox and Safari.
  - Checks: tap-to-play, volume and mute, Hebrew captions in RTL with correct punctuation, audio-video and caption sync, understandable when muted, VoiceOver and TalkBack on the transcript, signed-URL expiry, offline and slow network.
  - Every row starts as **pending** and is marked passed only after it is actually done.
- `MEDIA_REQUIRED=true` only after the required catalog is published and verified.

### Existing plans and rollout
- Every production `ExerciseVersion` today is free-form with a null technique. It stays exactly as it is: instructions, criteria, attempts and mastery. It shows the honest "no demonstration" state. Compatibility is never inferred from keywords.
- **Locked future levels** can receive technique-bound content only through the owner-initiated revise_future flow (a new version).
- **The current level** can receive it only through adapt: two struggles, or a safety suspension with an eligible replacement.
- **Completed levels** never change.

## B. Production track (paid steps only after owner approval)

**Vendor proposal.** Prices come from secondary aggregators (2026) and must be confirmed on the official pages before buying.
- **Video.** Google Veo 3.1 Fast through the Gemini API, about $0.10–0.12 per second at 720p–1080p. Alternatives are Kling 3.0 (about $0.08–0.17 per second) and Runway Gen-4 Turbo (about $0.05 per second). Generate the video **without speech** and add the voice in post-production, so the Hebrew is exact and no voices compete.
- **Voice.** Azure AI Speech neural TTS with he-IL voices, about $15–16 per 1M characters, with a free monthly tier; the voice names need checking. The alternative is ElevenLabs multilingual (about $0.10 per 1K characters; check which models support Hebrew). Use two distinct voices: narrator and demonstrator.
- **Assembly.** Local ffmpeg (free): mix, H.264 and AAC, `+faststart`.
- **Estimate.** Pilot clip: 20 s × about 5 takes ≈ 100 s of video ≈ **$10–15** (Veo Fast), plus TTS under $1. Remaining 11 clips at about 4 takes each ≈ **$90–110**, plus TTS of about $1–3.
- **Order.** Produce **one pilot clip** (`mouthing-redirect-toy@1` STANDARD) for owner review before batching the rest.

**Clip specification:**
- MP4, H.264 plus AAC stereo, -16 LUFS ±1 for narration, faststart, 1280×720, 12–25 s, at most 15 MB.
- Narration ends at least 0.3 s before each demonstrator line. Captions cover all speech, and there is no burned-in text.
- Muted, the captions alone must convey every instruction.

**Precise versus illustrative.**
- Illustrative: setting and props.
- Precise, verified frame by frame and rejected on failure: hand still on mouthing; toy offered while mouthing; praise while chewing the toy; the name spoken once, then the treat on the look.

**Technical checklist:** playback on reference devices; an audio track is present; loudness; intelligible Hebrew pronunciation; voice and action within ±0.3 s; captions match and sync with the speech; no AI artifacts; subjects fully in frame.

**Content checklist:** each core step is demonstrated in order and matches the rendered instructions; speech cues are audible at the right moment; no aversive handling; the puppy shows no fear; the sources were re-read and support every step.

## C. Acceptance (Phase 3)
1. ADMIN only, checked server-side from the database, for every admin read and write.
2. Publication needs TECHNICAL and CONTENT approval on the exact bundle fingerprint, plus an approved technique definition.
3. Content is immutable, and a change means a new version.
4. Mandatory instructions come from the catalog, and parameters are validated deterministically.
5. Free-form generation keeps working while the catalog is empty.
6. Withdrawal and suspension never alter exercise versions, attempts or mastery events.
7. A suspension is atomic with respect to mastery and generation commits.
8. Adapt is never attempted without an eligible replacement.
9. Playback makes no progression writes and no AI calls.
10. Captions, transcript, audio sync and error states work.
11. Real-device checks are recorded honestly as pending or done.
